A note on the provision text included in questions/. Excerpts of Korean
statutes and administrative rules appear as gold evidence in the question
files. Under Article 7 of the Korean Copyright Act, statutes, decrees,
ordinances and rules are excluded from copyright protection, so no
third-party license attaches to them. The CC BY 4.0 grant above covers the
selection, annotation, and arrangement of that material, which is the
authorial contribution here.
